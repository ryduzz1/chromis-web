Privacy Policy

Chromis is designed as a local creative tool. Your clips, looks, app preferences, and exports stay under your control.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Overview

Chromis does not use third-party tracking. The app stores settings such as codec, resolution, frame rate, dynamic range, monitor assists, haptics, audio preferences, LUT selections, and project state so the workspace can behave consistently between launches.

Media And Project Data

Videos, imported LUT files, saved looks, export files, and camera tracking packages are handled on the device unless you choose to export, save, share, or move them using system tools.

Device Access

Chromis uses device capabilities required for camera recording, audio recording when enabled, storage availability checks, and local preference storage. These capabilities support the product features and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Website Data

This static website does not include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, sign-in forms, or account creation. If that changes in the future, this policy should be updated before those features are shipped.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Chromis developer at contact@chromis.app.
